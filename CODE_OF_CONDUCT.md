# Code of conduct

We want SolaceFi to be a welcoming project for people of every background and experience level.

**Do:** be respectful, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback, and credit others' work.
**Don't:** harass, insult, discriminate, publish others' private information, or push people to share financial or personal details.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems to Solacefipay@gmail.com with the subject "SolaceFi conduct". Reports are kept confidential.
